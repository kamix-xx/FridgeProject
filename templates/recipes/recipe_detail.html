The "Midnight in Seville"
Oranges

est. time: 10 h

current status: private

Ingredients:

  • • Sicilian Blood Oranges 67 pcs.
  • • Oranges with Stems and Leaves 21 pcs.
  • • Orange Blossom Honey 37 L
  • • Grand Marnier or Cointreau 42 L
  • • Rose Water 2 L

Description:

An elegant recipe for scrumptious oranges with or without leaves.

This isn't just a fruit plate; it's a masterclass in citrus minimalism. By stripping away the mundane "peel and pith," you transform a common supermarket staple into a glistening, jewel-toned centerpiece. Whether you've managed to find oranges with their pristine leaves attached or you're working with "naked" fruit, the secret lies in the contrast between the cold, sharp citrus and the warm, aromatic syrup.

Step 1:

The "Orange Surgery"
(The Supreme Cut)

Forget peeling; we're performing an extraction. To achieve "scrumptious" status, you must transform the fruit into Supremes - naked, glowing wedges free from all bitter white pith and membranes.

  1. Behead & Base: Slice off the top and bottom until you see the vibrant flesh.
  2. The Shave: Curve your knife from top to bottom, stripping away the peel and all white pith. You want a bald, glistening sphere.
  3. The Extraction: Slide your knife between the membranes to pop out individual wedges.
  4. Save the Gold: Squeeze the leftover "carcass" over your wedges for a natural bath of juice.
